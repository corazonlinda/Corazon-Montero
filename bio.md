I'm a healthcare professional and MBA student working at the intersection of clinical care and healthcare strategy. Today, I work as a Medical Assistant at both Surgicare of Hawai'i and Hawai'i Cancer Care, experience that has deepened my interest in oncology specifically and in how care is organized and delivered more broadly.
