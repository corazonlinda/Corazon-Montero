# AI Conventions

This file is the canonical source of truth for how AI tools should work with me in this repository. `CLAUDE.md` just points here.

## How I want explanations

I want all explanations to be explained like you are explaining to a 4th grade reading level child. I want them to be concise and give reasoning first and then the explanation after.  Give examples. As for results make sure to show the math or work behind it. Don't just post results without explanation. 

## What an AI may draft for me

AI may make outlines or drafts. AI may draft outlines or make drafts but it may not make any final decisions. Drafts are meant to assist in the learning process, not do all of the work for me.

## What an AI may not draft for me

AI may not make final recommendations or judgment calls. All final decisions must ran by me first. I have the final say for what to commit or any changes to be made. No changes or commits can be made without my final review. 

## What must never be pasted into a model

Patient information should never be pasted into a model.
