Re: Recommended Planting Plan

Based on the results of my model, I recommend planting 10 tomato beds, 20 carrot beds, and 30 mesclun beds. This mix gives the farm the highest profit while staying within its available resources. Even though tomatoes bring in the most revenue per bed, planting more than 10 would cost more than the additional revenue those beds would bring in. Carrots and mesclun, on the other hand, are both planted up to their limits.

If the farm has the opportunity to expand growing space for one of these crops, I would recommend adding carrot beds first. One additional carrot bed could increase profit by about $353, compared to about $247 for an additional mesclun bed. Because of this, I would not recommend spending more than about $353 for the space needed for one additional carrot bed, since anything above that would cost more than the extra profit it could bring in.

The main thing that would make me reconsider the 10/20/30 planting plan is a change in the price of tomatoes. The model stops at around 10 tomato beds because adding another bed would cost more than the $8,800 in revenue it would bring in. If tomato prices change, the number of tomato beds that are worth planting could also change. Because of this, I would check tomato prices before committing to the final planting plan for the season.
