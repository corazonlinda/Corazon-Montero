# Economic Research

This capability uses sourced evidence and simple economic calculations (expected loss and break-even analysis) to evaluate who bears the cost of a risk and whether the financial protections in place are enough to cover it. The method is described in [spec.md](spec.md).

**Exercised in:** Individual Research Paper: Dakota Access Pipeline — I used this capability to examine who would pay for an oil spill at the Lake Oahe crossing and whether the federal liability limit would cover the estimated damages. The [research brief](../../docs/briefs/2026-10-09-research-brief.md) and the [spec](spec.md) show my sources and calculations.
